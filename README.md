# 🎮💣 Jinx Arcade Bot

> *“Rules are meant to be broken... like buildings! Or people!”*  
> — **Jinx**, from *Arcane: League of Legends*

Welcome to **Jinx Arcade Bot**, a Telegram bot inspired by the chaotic, fun-loving spirit of Jinx!  
It brings **moderation, quotes, games, and group utilities** to your Telegram chats — all wrapped in a spark of madness. ⚡

---

## 🧠 Features

🔥 **Moderation Commands**
- `/warn` – Warn misbehaving members  
- `/mute` & `/unmute` – Control the chaos  
- `/ban` & `/unban` – Clean up your group  
- `/purge` – Instantly delete unwanted messages  

💬 **Quote System**
- `/quote` – Get a random quote  
- `/getquote` – Retrieve specific saved quotes  
- `/quotes` – List all saved quotes  

🎉 **Event Handlers**
- Auto welcome new members  
- Say goodbye to leaving users  
- Respond smartly to text messages
